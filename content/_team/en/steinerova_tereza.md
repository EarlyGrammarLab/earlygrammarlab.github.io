*child language development, eye-tracking;*

I am currently completing my Master’s degree in [Theoretical and Research Psychology at Charles University](https://www.studujfhs.com/tvp). I am also working at EAGLeLab, where I first became involved through an internship and practical training. I am now primarily involved in coordinating eye-tracking laboratory activities and conducting experiments using eye-tracking methods.

My research interests lie in developmental and experimental psychology, particularly in child development, language acquisition and processing, pre-literacy skills, early reading, and predictive language processing. I am especially interested in the mechanisms children use to understand language and in experimental approaches to studying cognitive and language development. My research experience has also included work on the home literacy environment and shared reading in families with preschool-aged children.

I plan to further develop these research interests during my PhD studies.
