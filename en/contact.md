## Where to find us

Our laboratory is a part of the Prague branch of Institute of Psychology, Czech Academy of Sciences, **temporarily** located at the following address:

Pod Vodárenskou věží 1143/4<br>
182 00 Praha 8

The institute is expected to move back to its original downtown residence in Hybernská during **2027**.

The institute is near the Ládví metro station. Please note that navigation systems often lead users to the back side of the building with a non-public closed parking lot. Enter the building using the main front-side entrance and wait for us at the reception.
